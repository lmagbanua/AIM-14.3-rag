## Working at Sebby Corp

### Remote work policy

Sebby Corp is a remote-first company. Employees may work from anywhere within
their country of employment. Teams hold a mandatory core-hours overlap of
11:00–15:00 in the company's home timezone (US Eastern) for meetings and pair
work. Employees who want to work abroad for more than 30 consecutive days must
get written approval from People Ops beforehand.

### Office access

The Toronto and Austin offices are open to all employees. Desks are first come,
first served; meeting rooms can be booked through the facilities portal. Badge
access requests go to facilities@sebby.example and are processed within two
business days.

## Time off and leave

### Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off per year, accrued monthly,
plus statutory public holidays. Unused PTO up to 5 days rolls over into the
first quarter of the next year and expires on March 31. PTO requests should be
filed in Workday at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three
days.

### Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. No doctor's
note is required for absences of up to three consecutive days. Longer illnesses
move to the short-term disability process managed by People Ops.

### Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave; secondary caregivers
receive 6 weeks. Leave can be taken in up to two blocks within the first year
after birth or adoption.

## Money matters

### Expense reports

Submit expenses through Expensify within 30 days of purchase. Receipts are
required for any expense over $25. Expenses over $500 need pre-approval from
your manager. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run after approval.

### Equipment budget

Every employee gets a one-time $1,500 home-office budget on joining, plus a
$500 refresh budget every two years. Laptops are provisioned by IT and are not
part of this budget.

## Security basics

### Passwords and 2FA

All company accounts must use the company password manager and have two-factor
authentication enabled. Hardware security keys are mandatory for engineers with
production access; IT issues two keys per person.

### Reporting incidents

Suspected phishing or account compromise must be reported to
security@sebby.example within one hour of discovery. Do not forward suspicious
emails to colleagues; use the "Report phish" button in the mail client.
